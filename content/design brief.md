A design brief is an outline of the product, they are usually provided by [[clients]]. A design brief allows you to plan out your research.

Design briefs usually include any requirements that the client has for the product and any limitations they wish to set.

Briefs can be [open](open-briefs.md) or [closed](closed-briefs.md)

The design brief is the first step in [[starting-a-project]], and a main informer of required [[research]]

Definition

A design brief is the starting point for a design. It explains what should be included in the design and whatever problem needs to be solved.

Example

- A company which specialises in children’s toys has asked for a new and innovative design for a learning toy for a toddler.
- It should include the opportunity for the child to learn about numbers, letters, colours or a combination of these elements.
- The toy should be inspired by the [[theme]] ‘in the wild’, have some form of movement included and develop the child’s hand eye coordination.
