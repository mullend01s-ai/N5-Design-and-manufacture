Materials are the physical substance used to create a product.

Any material can be used however different materials and [[material-properties]] suit certain applications.

At N5 the three main categories of material discussed are

[[wood ]]is a material Derived from Trees. It can come in [[hardwood]] or [[softwood]]. 

[[Plastic]] is a synthetic material made from long chains of molecules called polymers, which are derived mostly from fossil fuels like crude oil, natural gas, or coal.

[[Metal]]A metal is a material that is typically hard, shiny, opaque, and a good conductor of heat and electricity. [[1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OB6QfKJebok)]

Material selection can influence a products [functionality](function), [safety](safety)
, and [appearance](aesthetics)

. Making selecting appropriate materials crucial to the designer.
