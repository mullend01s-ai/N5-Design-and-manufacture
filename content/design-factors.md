Design factors are the any specific constraint, requirement, or variable that engineers and designers must consider to ensure a product is functional, safe, economical, and appealing.(https://www.bbc.co.uk/bitesize/guides/zm4mmsg/revision/4), 

Understanding the factors that influence a design can help us understand existing products, and also understand what we should take into account when designing new products.
