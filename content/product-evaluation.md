A PRODUCT EVALUATION AIMS TO ENHANCE UNDERSTANDING AND GATHER
VALUABLE INSIGHTS FOR IMPROVED DESIGN. DESIGNERS SHOULD DOCUMENT THEIR
EVALUATION PROCESS AND RESULTS, EVEN IF INCONCLUSIVE, USING EVIDENCE
LIKE PHOTOGRAPHS, VIDEOS, AND QUESTIONNAIRES. ENSURING THE RESEARCH IS
MEANINGFUL AND WELL-DOCUMENTED IS ESSENTIAL FOR EFFECTIVE EVALUATION.
